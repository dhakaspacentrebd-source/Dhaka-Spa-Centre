## Cannibalization controls

The homepage owns brand discovery. Dhaka owns citywide planning; Gulshan owns neighbourhood orientation; Gulshan 2 owns the actual arrival address. Banani and Baridhara guides explicitly describe travel to Gulshan 2, not branches. Nearby search does not infer visitor location. Services compares techniques, prices compares costs, and treatment pages answer technique-specific questions. Journal articles focus on preparation and comparisons rather than duplicating commercial pages.

Natural spelling variants (centre/center, spa Dhaka/Dhaka spa) need no duplicate routes. No unsupported “best” or “number one” claims. The old aroma-body-massage path redirects permanently to aromatherapy-massage and is excluded from the sitemap. Titles/descriptions are unique; all canonical origins come from NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL.
