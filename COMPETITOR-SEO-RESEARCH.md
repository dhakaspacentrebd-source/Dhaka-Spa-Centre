## Discrepancies and decisions

The scrub page shows a scrub heading at ৳6,600 but duration text at ৳6,000/৳8,000. Its B2B heading shows ৳5,200 while the detailed row shows ৳7,500/৳10,000. The dedicated price list has no scrub entry. Therefore scrub remains **Enquire**; B2B follows the dedicated list.

The existing local project had unrelated NAP details, unsupported opening hours/coordinates and a different 12-treatment price menu without evidence of owner approval. Corrected NAP to the supplied brief. Retained existing treatment paths as enquiry options; unverified prices are no longer presented as fixed rates. Renamed aroma-body-massage to aromatherapy-massage with a permanent redirect. Added full body, Swedish, foot and scrub guides. All availability and guide prices require business confirmation.

## Original opportunities implemented

- Give every treatment its own useful technique, preparation and comfort guidance.
- Distinguish a menu page, price-comparison page and individual treatment intent.
- Differentiate location guides by visitor task: city planning, wider Gulshan orientation, exact Road 90 arrival, travel from Banani/Baridhara, and user-controlled nearby search.
- Use contextual links from guides to relevant treatments, prices and contact information.
- Answer questions about reservation confirmation, session length, products, pressure, address and arrival.
- Publish no invented ratings, branches, amenities, credentials, opening hours or medical outcomes.
- Keep offer and specialist availability wording factual; do not borrow promotional discounts.

Competitor observations are qualitative, not keyword-volume research. Search Console and a verified Business Profile are needed for post-launch performance analysis.
